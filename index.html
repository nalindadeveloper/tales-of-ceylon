About the island

One island. A thousand stories.

Sri Lanka packs rainforest, cloud-draped mountains, ancient cities and more than 1,300 kilometres of coastline into an island smaller than Ireland. Two and a half thousand years of recorded history, Buddhist, Hindu, Muslim and Christian traditions, and a climate that shifts from monsoon-soaked south-west to sun-baked north make every journey here feel like several.

I.

Culture

Drummers, dancers and festivals such as Kandy's Esala Perahera keep centuries-old traditions vividly alive.

II.

Landscapes

Within a day you can trade palm-lined southern bays for misty tea country two kilometres above the sea.

III.

Heritage

Eight UNESCO World Heritage Sites span ancient royal capitals, cave temples, a sacred city and a colonial fort.
